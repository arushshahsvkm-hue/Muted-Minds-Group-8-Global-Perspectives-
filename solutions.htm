Building Healthy Digital Habits

Practical, actionable steps to cultivate digital autonomy without full isolation.

1. Curate Your Feed

Unfollow or mute accounts that trigger negative self-comparison. Actively follow profiles that inspire hobbies, learning, or genuine offline interests.

2. Tech-Free Sleep Zones

Keep phones away from the bed overnight. Replacing late-night screen time protects restorative sleep cycles and mood regulation.

3. Silence Non-Essential Alerts

Push notifications use unpredictable rewards to pull focus. Disabling notification badges restores control over when you choose to open apps.

4. Schedule Unplugged Hours

Reserve specific time blocks each day for focused studying, hobbies, or face-to-face social time without secondary screen distractions.
