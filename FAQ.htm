Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about adolescent psychology and online habits.

General Mental Health & Social Media

Is social media inherently harmful to adolescents?
No. Platforms offer significant opportunities for community building, creative expression, and staying connected with friends. Harm usually correlates with excessive screen time, algorithmic rabbit holes, or severe social comparison.
What is social comparison bias?
It occurs when users measure their personal achievements or appearance against the edited highlights of others online, leading to feelings of inadequacy.

Sleep, Focus & Physiology

How does night-time screen use affect sleep quality?
Blue light suppresses melatonin production (the sleep hormone), making it harder to fall asleep. Additionally, exciting or anxiety-inducing content keeps the brain alert when it should be unwinding.
What is "Doomscrolling" and why is it addictive?
Doomscrolling is the habit of continuously reading negative news or posts. It is fueled by evolutionary anxiety—our brains naturally seek out information about potential threats, even if it leaves us exhausted.
